# Brand DNA — Freeprop AI
*(Freeprop AI — short for "Freelance Property with AI")*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Freeprop AI is a **digital operating system for property agents** — a solution that helps property agents (solo or team/agency) manage listings, build teams, and produce promotional content automatically and professionally, without needing to be a designer, video editor, or digital marketer.

The product bridges two realities of the Indonesian property industry:
- **Solo agents** working alone, with limited time and content skills
- **Team/agency agents** who need coordination across freelance agents, a centralized listing database, and brand consistency across all promotional content

---

## 2. Purpose — Why This Product Exists

> Property agents in Indonesia spend more time on manual listing entry, photo editing, and caption writing than on actually closing deals. Freeprop AI exists so agents can get their time back where it belongs: **meeting clients, negotiating, closing.**

---

## 3. Vision & Mission

**Vision:**
To become the number-one digital infrastructure for property agents in Indonesia — from solo agents in small towns to agencies with dozens of freelance agents.

**Mission:**
- Simplify listing management (manual entry + auto-scraping from third-party sources)
- Automate promotional content production (calendar, visuals, video scripts, captions)
- Provide a professional structure for agent teams (recruitment, brand profile, collaboration)
- Empower solo agents to look as professional as large teams

---

## 4. Core Values

| Value | What it means for the product |
|---|---|
| **Efficiency without compromising quality** | Automation doesn't mean cheap-looking output — it still looks professional |
| **Flexible for solo & teams** | One system, two working modes — never forces a team structure on a solo agent |
| **Listing data transparency** | The source of every listing (manual/scraped) is always clear, never pulled carelessly |
| **Local & contextual** | Built for the Indonesian property market context — not a generic template from abroad |
| **The agent as the trusted figure** | Generated content strengthens the agent's/agency's personal branding — it doesn't replace them |

---

## 5. Brand Positioning Statement

> For Indonesian property agents — solo or team-based — who want to manage listings and promotion without the hassle, Freeprop AI is a property operating system that combines listing management (manual & automated scraping) with a promotional content engine (calendar, AI visuals, video scripts, captions) in one place. Unlike property marketplaces (Rumah123, OLX) that only serve as a storefront, Freeprop AI is the **behind-the-scenes work tool for agents** that makes them look professional in front of clients.

## 7. Product Pillars (Feature-to-Brand Narrative Map)

*4 pillars: Team Building, Listing Intelligence, Content Engine, Lead Capture & Follow-Up*

### Pillar 1 — Team Building & Brand Setup
- Recruit/invite freelance agents into a team
- Set up an agent brand profile (for teams) / register identity & region scope (for solo agents)
- **Brand narrative:** *"Your team, your identity, in one clear profile."*

### Pillar 2 — Listing Intelligence
- Manual listing entry
- Auto-scraping from external sources (Acehome.com, OLX, Rumah123, etc.)
- All data automatically stored in a centralized database
- **Brand narrative:** *"One database, every listing source — manual or automatic, always organized."*

### Pillar 3 — Content Engine (Promotion Production)
- Content calendar (per listing or across an agent's/team's entire listing portfolio)
- Professional visual/image generation (AI-generated)
- Video scripts & storyboard scripts (not a fully rendered video — focused on script & storyboard)
- Caption content, produced collaboratively with a team or consumed solo
- **Brand narrative:** *"From listing to post-ready content — calendar, visuals, video scripts, captions, automated."*

### Pillar 4 — Lead Capture & WhatsApp Follow-Up
- Collect leads from promotional content/listing landing pages into one place (no more scattered DMs or comments)
- WhatsApp chatbot to handle initial buyer questions (price, specs, viewing schedule, etc.)
- Scheduled automatic follow-up for leads who haven't closed yet (nurturing, reminders, similar-unit offers)
- Integrated via a WhatsApp API/gateway (e.g. WAHA or a similar provider) — the agent can always take over the chat once a lead is "warm"
- **Brand narrative:** *"Leads come in, the chatbot answers first, follow-up runs itself — the agent steps in at the right moment."*

### Pillar 5 Landing Page generator
- Create generator static page that can insert with Meta Pixel or Tiktok Pixel or Google Ads
- Landing page can edit by a prompt (free user can only limited with 3 listings can be generated), and as teams unlimited

## 8. Brand Personality

**Blended archetype:** *The Sage (trusted expert)* + *The Ruler/Organizer (structure-giver)*

**Brand traits:**
- Organized & systematic (not messy like a manual spreadsheet)
- Supportive, not preachy — the brand acts as an "assistant," not a "boss"
- Confident but grounded — speaks the everyday language of property agents
- Efficient without feeling "robotic" — output still feels personal/human

**Brand tone in one line:**
> "Like having a personal assistant who understands the property business — not complicated software that gives you a headache."

---

## 9. Brand Voice & Tone

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product UI/Notifications | Clear, concise, actionable | "3 new listings from OLX have been added to your database. Check them now." |
| Chat/WhatsApp to agents | Friendly, personal, collaborative | "This week's content calendar is ready — just approve it." |
| Social media captions (output) | Adapts to each agent's/team's style | Tailored to each agent's own brand — not one rigid voice |
| Onboarding/product marketing | Confident, problem-solving | "Tired of manual listings and stressing over captions? We've got it covered." |

**Do:**
- Use a casual-professional tone matching how local property agents actually communicate
- Focus on real outcomes (time saved, organized listings, ready-to-use content)

**Don't:**
- Overload on AI technical jargon ("neural network," "machine learning pipeline")
- Overclaim ("100% automated, zero human touch") — always position the agent as the main figure, with the tool as support

## 13. Visual Identity Direction (Direction, Not Final Design)

- **Mood:** professional, trustworthy, modern — but still approachable (not stiff/corporate)
- **Suggested color palette:** dark navy/blue (trust, professionalism) combined with a warm accent color (orange/golden yellow) to evoke "property = home, warmth"
- **Typography:** modern sans-serif for UI (clean, easy to read on a dashboard), with the option to add a thin serif accent for high-end promotional material (luxury home listings)
- **Visual elements:** simplified house/key/map icons (minimalist), avoiding the generic property clipart overused by marketplaces
- **Usability:** support dark/light mode by switch icon, the color scheme follow `ui-ux-pro-max` skills. and all setup color and font insert in Tailwindcss config

## 13. Differentiation vs. Competitors/Data Sources

| Player | Their position | Freeprop AI's position |
|---|---|---|
| Rumah123, OLX, Acehome.com | Marketplace/storefront for buyers | A scraped data source, not a competitor — we operate behind the agent's scenes |
| Generic property CRM software | Focused on data & pipeline only | We combine listing data + a promotional content engine into one |
| Freelance designers/social media services | Manual, slow, project-based | We're automated and integrated with real-time listing data |
| Generic WhatsApp chatbot/CRM tools (e.g. WAHA-based, Qontak, etc.) | Focused on chat & leads only, separate from listing data | Our chatbot & follow-up connect directly to the listing and content database — chatbot replies are always based on the latest listing info |
